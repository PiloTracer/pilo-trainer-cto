# Engineering Leadership

**Slug:** `engineering-leadership`  
**Duration:** 6–8 weeks · 2 sessions/week  
**Level:** CTO / VP Eng with team ≥5

## Audience / level assumptions

- Leads through other people; individual output is no longer the job
- Owns at least one org-design or hiring decision in the current quarter
- Has felt the pull to reorganise instead of decide

## Outcomes (measurable)

1. Org design mapped to the four team types with interaction modes named
2. Hiring scorecard and interview loop for one critical seat
3. Written performance case, including the decision to part ways
4. One culture mechanism (not a poster) with an owner and a review date
5. Levelling expectations for your own team, adapted rather than copied

## Duration & cadence

6–8 weeks, 2 sessions/week plus one async drill.

## Modules

### M1 — Org design

- **Objectives:** Structure follows flow and cognitive load, not org-chart fashion. Every team maps to exactly one of the four types; every dependency gets an interaction mode.
- **Drill:** `org-design` — case 5, redraw a 25-engineer org carrying three unrelated domains plus the pipeline
- **Sources:** Team Topologies key concepts, Skelton & Pais — https://teamtopologies.com/key-concepts; free summary — https://itrevolution.com/articles/four-team-types/; interaction modelling — https://teamtopologies.com/key-concepts-content/team-interaction-modeling-with-team-topologies
- **Exit check:** Names the cognitive load moved and to whom. A redraw that relabels without moving responsibility fails.

### M2 — Levelling and expectations

- **Objectives:** Define what each level means by scope and impact, so promotion is a judgement about evidence rather than tenure
- **Drill:** Write level expectations for two adjacent levels on your team; the professor tests them against two real people
- **Sources:** progression.fyi — https://progression.fyi/; Dropbox Engineering Career Framework — https://dropbox.github.io/dbx-career-framework/; Rent the Runway ladder — https://progression.fyi/f/rent-the-runway
- **Exit check:** Expectations describe scope and impact, not a task checklist; adapted to your context rather than pasted

### M3 — Hiring

- **Objectives:** Scorecard before pipeline; decide what you are willing to be wrong about
- **Drill:** `hire-plan` — case 10, one req against two critical gaps
- **Sources:** *Who: The A Method for Hiring*, Geoff Smart & Randy Street (Unverified page); public engineering hiring guides — cite the specific URL at drill time
- **Exit check:** Scorecard plus an explicit statement of which gap stays open and its compensating control

### M4 — Performance and exits

- **Objectives:** Calibration, direct feedback, and the decision to part ways
- **Drill:** Underperformer after two quarters — write the plan and the conversation opener
- **Sources:** *Radical Candor*, Kim Scott (Unverified page) — principles, not slogans; `standards/anti-patterns.md` § Organisation
- **Exit check:** Plan has a date, an observable bar, and a stated consequence. "More coaching" is not a plan.

### M5 — Culture as system

- **Objectives:** Rituals, on-call norms, and quality bars encoded as mechanisms
- **Drill:** Pick one culture failure mode; design the mechanism that makes it visible
- **Sources:** `standards/anti-patterns.md`; Google *SRE* ch. 16 On-call — https://sre.google/sre-book/table-of-contents/
- **Exit check:** Mechanism has an owner, a trigger, and a review date

## Assessment / rubrics

| Criterion | Pass |
|-----------|------|
| Org design | Load moved, not relabelled; interaction modes explicit |
| Levelling | Scope and impact based; adapted to context |
| Hiring | Scorecard precedes pipeline; scarcity allocated honestly |
| Performance | Observable bar, date, and consequence |
| Culture | A mechanism with an owner, not an aspiration |

Drill dimensions per `standards/assessment.md`: clarity, leverage, risk, stakeholder care,
learnability — average ≥3, no dimension at 1.

## Exit criteria (program complete)

All module exit checks met; artifacts stored under `.work.cto/` and linked from the task
ledger; `@cto-review status` run with evidence paths cited.
