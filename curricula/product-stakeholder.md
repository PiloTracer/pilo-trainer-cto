# Product & Stakeholder

**Slug:** `product-stakeholder`  
**Duration:** 4–6 weeks  
**Level:** CTO partnering with Product / CEO / board

## Audience / level assumptions

- Shares roadmap ownership with a product counterpart, or should and does not
- Writes or presents to a CEO or board on a recurring cadence
- Most likely failure mode on entry: status theatre — updates that report activity and force no decision

## Duration & cadence

4–6 weeks, 2 sessions/week plus one async drill.

## Outcomes

- Crisp product partnership model (discovery vs delivery)
- Exec updates that create decisions, not theater
- Board narrative: risk, capacity, bets — not vanity metrics

## Modules

### M1 — Product partnership
- Objectives: Shared outcomes, conflict protocols, roadmap ownership
- Drill: Rewrite a messy product/eng conflict into a decision memo
- Sources: Empowered (Cagan); Shape Up (Bach — Basecamp, public)

### M2 — Exec communication
- Objectives: Bottom line up front; options; ask
- Drill: `drills/case-library.md` case 6 — one-page CEO update carrying bad news
- Sources: Public writing on executive communication (name the specific URL at drill time); Pyramid Principle concepts; `standards/anti-patterns.md` § Stakeholders
- Exit check: Bad news in the first line; ≤3 decisions, ≤2 risks, exactly one ask

### M3 — Board / investor
- Objectives: Answer the question behind the question; defend a measurement position
- Drill: `board-qa` — case 7, delivery metrics under interrogation
- Sources: `standards/metrics.md`; DORA — https://dora.dev/research/; board practice writing from reputable VC/operator sources (cite the specific URL)
- Exit check: Throughput paired with stability; one blind spot named; no stale benchmark tier quoted as current

## Assessment / rubrics

| Criterion | Pass |
|-----------|------|
| Partnership | Conflict protocol named, not implied |
| Exec update | Forces a decision; bad news leads |
| Board answer | Honest about unknowns; metrics used correctly |

Pass if communications force a decision and surface risk honestly. Drill dimensions per
`standards/assessment.md`: average ≥3, no dimension at 1.

## Exit criteria (program complete)

Partnership charter, sample board appendix, and a passing drill score; artifacts stored under
`.work.cto/` and linked from the task ledger; `@cto-review status` run with evidence paths.
